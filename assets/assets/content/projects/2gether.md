2gether is an emotionally charged game about a mother monkey's struggle to escape a fire to protect her baby. The game features a unique tension-balance mechanic: players must maintain an ideal distance from her baby to prevent the tree branch from breaking under the weight (too close), but also to ensure the baby doesn't stop moving out of fear (too far).
