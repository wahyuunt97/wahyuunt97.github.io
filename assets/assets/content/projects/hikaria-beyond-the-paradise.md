Hikaria is a groundbreaking light show brand created through a collaboration between Timedoor Indonesia and Run-Hun Sha. Set in the beautiful landscape of Bali, Hikaria blends traditional storytelling with cutting-edge technology, offering an immersive experience where light, culture, and nature come together.

With Timedoor Indonesia’s expertise in digital innovation and Run-Hun Sha’s mastery in immersive entertainment, Hikaria delivers a unique visual experience through advanced projection mapping and interactive visuals, creating a magical journey for audiences of all ages.
